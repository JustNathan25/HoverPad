WS_EX_NOACTIVATE Topmost Surface

HoverPad requests the desktop window manager to map the floating surface with the WS_EX_NOACTIVATE bitmask flag. The foreground process (your terminal, editor, or browser) never receives a WM_KILLFOCUS notification.

CreateWindowExW( WS_EX_TOPMOST | WS_EX_NOACTIVATE | WS_EX_TOOLWINDOW, L"HoverPadMicaHost", nullptr, WS_POPUP, x, y, w, h, nullptr, nullptr, hInstance, nullptr );
